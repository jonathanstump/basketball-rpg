## Post-RC updates

| Date | Change | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-10-02 | **Tutorial controls clarity.** Every tutorial prompt now names the player's own key or button: `{action}` tokens resolve through `InputPrompts` from the live InputMap (Settings remaps), for the last-used device, and refresh on remap or device swap. The old prompts named no key for Interact or Shoot and had wrong defaults (crossover said Space, which is Jump; Quarter Water said R, which is Interact). A hint under the prompt points to Pause → Settings → Controls. Settings → **Controls** lists every action (including movement) with its keyboard and controller binding side by side and what it does, and remaps it. The tutorial pause menu opens Controls directly. District and interior interact hints share the same lookup. 6 new tests (116 asserts), plus a `title_controls` render shot. | Rebinding a pad action to a trigger (axis) isn't supported; buttons only (unchanged from M13) |
| 2026-10-02 | **Tutorial no longer gets stuck.** (1) The on-screen prompt only refreshed after combat events, so the "Move… sprint" prompt stayed up forever after calling next. It now follows the open step every frame. (2) The move step completed instantly, because it was measured from the gate. It now needs moving about 4 m from where you called next and sprinting once. (3) The strip step was impossible: Hands Up is defense-only (spec §7), but the tutorial left the ball in your hands, and the crate hoop hands it back. The strip step now sets your ball aside (defense drill, prompt says so) and returns it after. (4) Three crew could chain-hit the player into permanent hit-stun; the tutorial now allows 1 attacker at a time (AttackTokenManager.cap). New tests: real-input walk-up and move-step repro, and a bot that plays the whole tutorial with real button presses (all 9 steps, about 15 s of game time). | The ankle-breaker is still the hardest step (timing on the flash), about 9 s even for the bot |
